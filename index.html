Programmer and language enthusiast. I enjoy exploring how software can most practically model and affect reality. I enjoy functional languages, especially the Lisp family, and adapting their principles for building software with native-compiled systems languages. I enjoy extracting maximum performance from abstractions which simplify, such as Nim's single-ownership value semantics.
